# Weather-Based Clothing Guide

## Temperature guide
- Above 38°C (very hot): light-coloured, loose cotton clothing, a hat or cloth over the head, sunglasses, and sunscreen.
- 30°C to 38°C (hot): breathable, lightweight fabrics such as cotton or linen. Avoid dark colours and synthetic fabrics.
- 20°C to 29°C (pleasant): normal light clothing. Carry a light layer for the evening.
- 10°C to 19°C (cool): a light jacket, sweater, or long sleeves.
- 5°C to 9°C (cold): a warm jacket, layers, and a scarf.
- Below 5°C (very cold): heavy coat, thermal inner layer, gloves, warm cap, and warm socks.

## Rain
Carry an umbrella or wear a waterproof jacket. Wear shoes with good grip, avoid white or delicate fabrics, and keep a spare dry pair of socks.

## High humidity
Wear loose, breathable, moisture-wicking clothing. Tight and synthetic clothes trap sweat and feel uncomfortable.

## Strong wind
Wear a windproof jacket, tie back long hair, and avoid loose scarves or very light clothing.

## Sun and UV
Use sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher on exposed skin, wear sunglasses, and cover your head between 11 AM and 4 PM.

## Fog and smog
Wear a mask in poor air quality and carry something that protects your eyes. Warm layers help during cold, foggy mornings.
