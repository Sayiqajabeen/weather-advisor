# Weather and Farming Advice

## Hot and dry weather (above 38°C)
- Irrigate crops early in the morning or in the evening to reduce evaporation.
- Use mulching to keep soil moisture.
- Avoid spraying pesticides or fertilizer in the heat, because it can burn leaves.
- Provide shade, clean water, and ventilation for livestock. Heat stress reduces milk and egg production.

## Heavy rain and high humidity
- Make sure fields have drainage to prevent waterlogging, which damages roots.
- High humidity increases fungal diseases such as blight, rust, and mildew. Inspect crops regularly.
- Delay spraying and fertilizer application until the rain stops, or they will wash away.
- Harvest ripe crops before heavy rain if possible, and store grain in dry, ventilated places.

## Strong wind (above 10 m/s)
- Avoid spraying, because the wind causes drift.
- Support tall crops such as sugarcane, maize, and banana plants.
- Secure greenhouse covers, tunnels, and loose equipment.

## Cold weather and frost (below 5°C)
- Frost can damage vegetables, fruit, and young plants. Cover sensitive crops overnight.
- Light irrigation in the evening helps protect crops, because moist soil holds heat longer.

## Moderate weather (20°C to 30°C, low wind, no rain)
This is generally good for field work such as sowing, harvesting, spraying, and transplanting.

## Livestock
Provide fresh water at all times, shelter from extreme heat, cold, and rain, and watch animals for signs of stress.
