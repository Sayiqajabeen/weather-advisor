# Heavy Rain, Monsoon and Flood Safety

## Rain intensity guide
- Light rain: less than 2.5 mm per hour. Normal activity is possible.
- Moderate rain: 2.5 to 7.5 mm per hour. Roads become slippery and visibility drops.
- Heavy rain: 7.5 to 50 mm per hour. Waterlogging in low-lying areas and urban flooding are possible.
- Very heavy rain: more than 50 mm per hour. Flash flood risk is high.

## Precautions during heavy rain
- Avoid travelling unless necessary. Drive slowly and keep a larger distance from other vehicles.
- Never walk or drive through flowing floodwater. Even 15 cm of moving water can knock a person down, and 60 cm can carry away a car.
- Stay away from electric poles, loose wires, and open drains or manholes.
- Keep mobile phones and power banks charged.
- Keep emergency supplies ready: drinking water, dry food, torch, first aid kit, and important documents in a waterproof bag.

## Flood warning signs
Rapidly rising water levels in rivers or drains, continuous heavy rain for several hours, and official flood alerts from local authorities. Move to higher ground early and do not wait for the water to reach your home.

## After a flood
- Drink only boiled or bottled water, because floodwater contaminates supplies.
- Avoid touching electrical appliances that were wet.
- Watch for waterborne diseases such as diarrhoea, typhoid, malaria, and dengue.

## Thunderstorms
During thunder and lightning, stay indoors, avoid open fields, tall trees, and metal objects, and unplug sensitive electronics.
