# Weather and Outdoor Activities

## Ideal conditions for outdoor activities
Temperature of 15°C to 28°C, wind under 6 m/s, no rain, and low to moderate humidity. These conditions suit walking, jogging, cycling, cricket, football, and picnics.

## Hot weather (above 35°C)
- Avoid intense exercise between 11 AM and 4 PM.
- Exercise early in the morning or after sunset.
- Drink water before, during, and after activity.
- Stop immediately if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or weak.

## Cold weather (below 10°C)
- Warm up well before starting to avoid muscle injury.
- Wear layers that you can remove as your body warms up.

## Rain
Outdoor sports are not advised in heavy rain. Wet ground increases the risk of slips and injuries. Light drizzle is fine for walking if you wear suitable shoes.

## Strong wind
Avoid cycling, kite flying, and activities near trees or on rooftops when wind is strong. Wind above 10 m/s makes outdoor activity uncomfortable and sometimes unsafe.

## Thunderstorms
Stop all outdoor activity and go indoors. Wait at least 30 minutes after the last thunder before going back outside.

## High humidity
High humidity makes exercise feel harder, because sweat does not evaporate easily. Reduce intensity and take more breaks.

## Poor air quality
When air quality is poor, children, elderly people, and people with asthma should avoid outdoor exercise. Wear a mask if you must go out.

## Picnics, camping, and events
Check the forecast the day before, have an indoor backup plan, carry water, sun and rain protection, and avoid camping near rivers during the rainy season.
