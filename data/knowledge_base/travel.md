# Weather and Travel Advice

## Good travel conditions
Clear or partly cloudy sky, temperature between 15°C and 32°C, light wind, no rain, and good visibility. Travel is generally comfortable and safe.

## Rain and road travel
- Light rain: drive carefully and turn on headlights.
- Heavy rain: reduce speed, avoid sudden braking, and avoid underpasses and low-lying roads, which may flood.
- Allow extra time, because rain can cause traffic and delays.

## Fog and low visibility
- Use low-beam headlights and fog lights. Do not use high beams, which reflect off the fog.
- Maintain a safe distance and drive slowly.
- Motorway and highway closures are common in dense fog during winter mornings. Check conditions before leaving.

## Extreme heat travel
- Start journeys early in the morning or in the evening.
- Carry plenty of water, and check tyre pressure, coolant, and the battery before long drives.
- Never leave children or pets in a parked vehicle.
- Take breaks in shade or air-conditioned places.

## Strong wind and storms
- High-sided vehicles, motorcycles, and cyclists are most at risk in strong wind.
- Avoid travelling during thunderstorms. If caught on the road, park safely away from trees and electric poles.

## Air travel
Fog, dust storms, and thunderstorms are common causes of flight delays. Check the flight status before leaving for the airport.

## Before you travel
Check the forecast for both your starting point and destination, carry an umbrella and water, and keep your phone charged.
